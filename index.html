The short version

Your photos stay on your phone

Pictures and doodles you add are saved only inside the app on your device. We never upload them, see them or share them.

No account. No sign-up.

We don't ask for your name, email or any login. Just open it and start blasting.

Ads by Google AdMob

The game is free thanks to ads. Google AdMob may collect device data to show and measure ads, as explained below.

Scores via Game Center

Leaderboards run on Apple Game Center and are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

This Privacy Policy explains how Stickageddon (the "app") handles information. Stickageddon is made by Mark Rogers ("we", "us"). By using the app you agree to this policy.

1Photos, camera & your doodles

Stickageddon lets you turn pictures into characters (ships, bullets, enemies and bosses). To do that, the app asks permission to use your camera and/or your photo library.

  • We only access the photos you choose, when you choose them.
  • The images you add are stored only on your device, inside the app.
  • They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see them.
  • You can delete a character at any time from the Character Roster. Deleting the app removes them all.
You can change camera and photo permissions at any time in your iPhone's Settings › Stickageddon.

Please only add photos of people who are happy to be in your game.

2Advertising (Google AdMob)

Stickageddon is free and shows ads, including ads between games and optional ads you can watch to continue. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show, limit and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device type, operating system and app version
  • ad interactions, such as whether an ad was shown or tapped

If your iPhone asks whether Stickageddon can track you and you choose Ask App Not to Track, your advertising identifier won't be used to personalise ads. You may still see ads, but they won't be personalised that way. You can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3Leaderboards (Apple Game Center)

If you're signed in to Game Center, your scores and Game Center nickname may be submitted to Apple's leaderboards and shown to other players. This is handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. You can manage Game Center in Settings › Game Center.

4Game settings saved on your device

The app saves things like your best score, top wave, Chaos Mode and Flash settings on your device so they're remembered next time. This stays on your phone and isn't sent to us.

5What we don't do

  • We don't ask you to create an account or give us your name or email.
  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't upload or look at your photos or drawings.

6Children

Stickageddon is not specifically directed at children under 13. If you're a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we'll help remove it.

7Keeping & deleting information

Because your photos and game settings live on your device, you control them: delete characters in the app, or delete the app to remove everything. Information collected by Google or Apple is kept and deleted under their own policies linked above.

8Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the Australian Privacy Act, or the GDPR in the EU and UK), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information about you. We hold almost none, but if you have a request or question, contact us and we'll respond as soon as we can.

9Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add new features. We'll post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
